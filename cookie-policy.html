Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Clyxess AI uses cookies and similar technologies to recognize you when you visit our website.

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide reporting information.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for several reasons:

  • Essential Cookies: These are required for the operation of our website and to keep you logged in.
  • Analytics Cookies: These help us understand how users interact with our AI tools so we can improve the experience.
  • Functionality Cookies: These remember your preferences (like language or dark mode) to provide a personalized experience.

3. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. If you choose to block cookies, some parts of our AI service may not function properly.

This Cookie Policy explains how Clyxess AI ("We", "Us") uses cookies and similar technologies when you use our website and app. This policy is part of our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and keep you logged in securely.

2. How We Use Cookies - Minimal Use

Clyxess AI uses only ESSENTIAL and STRICTLY NECESSARY cookies. We do NOT use advertising, tracking, or third-party marketing cookies. We respect your privacy.

3. Third-Party Cookies

We use trusted processors like Supabase (for auth), Google (for OAuth), and Facebook (for OAuth). They may set their own essential cookies for authentication. We do not allow any third-party advertising cookies on Clyxess AI.

4. No Sale of Cookie Data

We do NOT sell, rent, or share data collected via cookies with any advertiser or data broker. Cookie data is used solely for functionality and security.

5. How to Control Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block cookies. However, if you block our essential authentication cookies, you will not be able to log in to Clyxess AI, as login requires Supabase session cookies. This is necessary for functionality.

6. Do Not Track

We respect Do Not Track (DNT) signals. Since we do not track you for advertising, DNT has no effect on our minimal cookie usage.
